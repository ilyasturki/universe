Universe already knows the buttons of most pads ([Supported controllers](/docs/controllers/supported/)). Set them up when a button lands in the wrong place or reads **Unbound**, or when Universe doesn't know the pad. What you set up holds for every pad of that model.

## Settings › Controller

Settings › [Controller](/docs/settings/controller/) shows the pad Universe reads, on a card named after its model. **A** on a button's row offers **Learn the button**, **On press…** and **On hold…** for its [macros](/docs/controllers/macros/), and **Clear press** or **Clear hold**.

<Shot name="settings-controller" />

The first time Universe sees a model you never set up, it offers to set up its buttons. **Not now** stops the question for that model.

## Set up the buttons

1. Press **A** on **Set up the buttons**. The pad's drawing fills the page, and the button it wants pulses, with a prompt such as "Press A".
2. Press that button, and Universe asks for the next one. A step you don't answer is skipped.
3. The standard buttons come first, then the model's extra buttons. Last, hold each stick right, then down.

Meanwhile the pad moves nothing on screen. Steer the setup from the keyboard, or click or tap the actions under the prompt: **Previous button (←)**, **Keep and finish (Enter)**, and **Cancel, undo all (Esc)**, which puts every button back. With a pad alone, let the remaining steps time out and the setup ends by itself.

## Learn one button

1. Press **A** on the button's row, then **Learn the button**.
2. When the row reads "Press the button on the pad…", press the button you want there.

Any button on that pad counts as the answer, **B** included, so stop with <kbd>Esc</kbd>. A pad with no HOME button can get one by learning the HOME row from a spare button.

## Test the buttons

**Test the buttons** shows what the pad sends. A press lights its button, and each stick and trigger shows how far it moves. A dashed button sends nothing on this connection: learn it from its row.

To leave the test, hold the right-hand face button for a second (**B** on an Xbox pad, ○ on a PlayStation pad, A on a Nintendo pad), press **Start** and **Select** together, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>.
