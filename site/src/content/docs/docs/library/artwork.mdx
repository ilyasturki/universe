Every game has six pictures: a **Box front**, a **Square**, a **Banner**, a **Background**, a **Logo** and a **Wallpaper**, the 16:9 picture that fills the launch screen. Universe fetches them when a game comes in, from the game's store, Steam, GOG GamesDB, libretro for an [emulated game](/docs/library/emulated/#artwork-for-emulated-games), and SteamGridDB once you add a key. The game's description and store screenshots come with them. A game that never had them fetched, such as one imported by an older version, gets them the next time Universe starts, and keeps the pictures it already has. A picture you pick always shows over the fetched one.

## The whole library

Settings › [Artwork](/docs/settings/artwork/) shows every game's pictures in one grid. **Fetch missing art**, Up from the first row, fills every game's empty pictures and keeps your picks.

## One game's art

The game menu (**Start** on a game) › **Manage** › **Artwork** shows the game's six pictures, each with where it shows. **A** on one opens its candidates from every source, and **A** on a candidate (**Use this**) makes it your pick.

**Start** there opens:

- **Fetch missing art**, for this game's empty pictures;
- **Use a file…**, for an image of your own;
- **Back to default** (**X**), which drops your pick;
- **Wrong game?** (**Y**), when the art is another game's.

## When the art is another game's

**Wrong game?** searches for the game's title. **A** (**Use this game**) takes a result, and **Y** (**Another name**) searches under another name. The pictures already fetched stay until you pick each one again.

## SteamGridDB

A key of your own from steamgriddb.com adds SteamGridDB's community art to the candidates. Enter it in Settings › **Game defaults** › **API keys**, shown with **Y**, or from the last cell of a game's candidates. **Prefer SteamGridDB art**, next to the key, fills each picture from SteamGridDB first.
