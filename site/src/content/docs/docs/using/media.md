Media, the third tab, holds the screenshots, recordings and journal entries of all your games, newest first. Each comes from a feature you turn on in Settings › **Features**: [Screenshots](/docs/add-ons/screenshots/), [Video capture](/docs/add-ons/video-capture/) and [Play journal](/docs/add-ons/play-journal/).

A game's own screenshots, recordings and journal open from the **Media** button on its page, the game menu's **Media ›** or the dock over the running game.

## The Media tab

- **A** on a screenshot shows it full screen, and Left and Right step through the others.
- **A** on a recording plays it, and **A** on a journal entry opens it.
- **X** opens the game's page, and **Y** jumps to the journal entry of a screenshot or a recording.
- **Start** › **Remove screenshot…**, **Remove recording…** or **Remove entry…** sends the item to the trash, after a question.

## Screenshots

[Playing](/docs/using/playing/#screenshots) says how to take one. Removing a screenshot also takes it out of the journal entry that shows it.

## Recordings

A game's **Recordings** page plays them: Left and Right seek and **X** goes full screen. **Start** › **Remove recording…** trashes the recording, with or without its journal entry. Your hours stay.

## The journal

After each session, the journal writes an entry from its recording and your screenshots: a title, a few paragraphs, and a **NEXT UP** line on where you left off. It needs a writing model first ([Play journal](/docs/add-ons/play-journal/)).

A game's **Journal** page lists every session. **A** reads its entry, or writes a missing one with **Write it**. **Start** adds **Write it again** and **Remove entry…**, which keeps your screenshots.

The Switch 2 and PS5 looks keep media in other places: see [Looks](/docs/using/looks/).
