Universe Big Screen has four tabs along the top: **Home**, **Library**, **Media** and **Settings**. **LB** and **RB** step through them. After the tabs come the search glass, the power button and, while a game runs, a badge with its title and how long it has been running.

Up from the top of a page reaches the tab bar, where landing on a tab switches to it. **A** opens search or the power menu, or resumes the game from its badge. Down goes back into the page. **B** does too on Home, and on any other tab returns to Home.

## Buttons and keys

These pages name the buttons by their Xbox letters. Each one but **HOME** has a key on the keyboard.

| Pad | Keyboard | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Plays, opens or picks what has the focus. Held on a game, it opens the game menu. |
| **B** | <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Goes back. Held, it opens the power menu. |
| **X** | <kbd>I</kbd> | Opens the focused game's page. |
| **Y** | <kbd>F</kbd> | Adds the focused game to your favourites, or takes it out. |
| **LB** / **RB** | <kbd>Q</kbd> / <kbd>E</kbd> | Previous and next tab. |
| **LT** / **RT** | <kbd>PgUp</kbd> / <kbd>PgDn</kbd> | Previous and next section in Settings, and on pages split into cards, such as a game's settings. |
| **Start** | <kbd>F1</kbd> | More, the menu for what has the focus. On a game, that's the game menu. |
| D-pad, left stick | Arrow keys | Move. |
| Right stick | <kbd>[</kbd> / <kbd>]</kbd> | Up and down move a screenful at a time. Left and right scrub through a recording. |
| **HOME** | none | Opens the dock over a running game ([Playing](/docs/using/playing/)). |

A hold lasts 450 ms. Where a button does something else, the hint bar says so. In Settings, **X** refreshes and **Y** turns a feature on or off.

<kbd>Backspace</kbd> goes back too, and <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> jump to the first and last item. The Select button (View, Create or − on your pad) and the stick clicks do nothing in the menus, though a [macro](/docs/controllers/macros/) can use them.

## The hint bar

The bar along the bottom lists the buttons the focused item takes, **A** first, and shows messages in its middle.

The buttons are drawn as your pad prints them:

- A PlayStation pad shows ✕ for **A**, ○ for **B**, □ for **X** and △ for **Y**, with L1, R1, L2 and R2 for the shoulders.
- An Xbox pad and a Steam Deck show the letters.
- On a Nintendo-style pad (a Switch Pro Controller, an 8BitDo Pro 3), each letter is the button printed with it, wherever it sits. **A**, on the right, confirms, and **B**, at the bottom, goes back.

With several pads connected, the hints follow the one connected first, or the one picked in Settings › **Controller**. Until Universe has seen a pad, they show Xbox letters, a Steam Deck's buttons on a Deck, or the pad picked in **Buttons and glyphs** in the [first-run setup](/docs/getting-started/first-run/). Pick up the keyboard or the mouse and they show key caps instead.

## Search

Search finds games and settings at once. Press **A** on the search glass in the tab bar, or click it.

<Shot name="search" />

- Games whose title contains what you type show as covers, last played first.
- Settings show under them: the section names, the rows of Game defaults and Controller, the runners, the features and the stores with their rows, Appearance's **Theme** and **Startup animation**, and each look by name.
- Games' own settings show once per setting, with how many games have it and how many override it. **A** on such a row expands it into one row per game.
- A word's start, a typo or a related word finds a row, and a word that names a game narrows the settings to that game's own.
- The sections' former names still find them: Launch (Game defaults), Runners (Emulators & Proton), Sources (Stores), Install (Downloads), Modules (Features), Themes (Appearance), System (Performance) and Doctor (System check).

On the on-screen keyboard, **X** deletes the last letter and **B** closes search from anywhere in it. Up from its top row reaches the results; a physical keyboard types straight in, and <kbd>Enter</kbd> moves to them. On a cover, **A** plays the game and **Start** opens its menu. On a setting, **A** opens its page with the row in view.

## Power

Hold **B** to open **Power**, or press **A** on the power button in the tab bar or on Settings › About › **Power**. Holding **B** does nothing while a game is launching or while a menu, a question or search is open.

<Shot name="power-menu" />

- **Quit Universe** closes Universe at once, and the running game with it. In the [Universe session](/docs/getting-started/universe-session/) it reads **Log out**.
- **Suspend** puts the machine to sleep. A running game keeps running.
- **Reboot** and **Power off** ask first, with the cursor on **Cancel**, and close the running game.
- **Stay** closes the menu.

Suspend, Reboot and Power off show only when logind allows them. Under [Steam's Game Mode](/docs/getting-started/steam-deck/#steams-game-mode) only Quit Universe and Stay remain; Steam's own menu has the power options.

## Menus and questions

In a menu, Right opens a row marked ›, Left or **B** goes back one level and **Start** closes the whole menu. Where a menu changes a game's setting, the hint bar can offer **A** for that game alone and **Y** for all your games.

In a question, **B** answers no. A click outside a menu or a question counts as **B**.

## Mouse and touch

- A click or a tap moves the focus to what it lands on, and a second one is **A**, so a second tap on a cover plays the game. Buttons, menu rows, tabs and the on-screen keyboard's keys act on the first tap.
- A press held past 180 ms on the focused item counts as a held **A**.
- Dragging and the wheel scroll. The wheel moves the home row sideways.
- Right click on a cover opens the game's page. Anywhere else, right click is **B**.

A finger counts as the pad, so the hints keep showing its buttons.

## In the other looks

The Switch 2 look puts a row of games over a bar of round buttons (**All Software**, **News**, **Install**, **Album**, **Controllers**, **System Settings** and **Power**). **B** goes back from any page, and **Start** returns to HOME wherever the page doesn't use it. Holding **B** opens **Power Options**, with **Quit Universe**, **Sleep Mode**, **Restart** and **Turn Off**. System Settings › **Search** finds settings only, and **All Software** has a search of its own for titles.

On the Switch 2 and PS5 keyboards, **B** deletes the last letter, and closes the keyboard once the field is empty; **X** closes it at once.

The PS5 look has two tabs, **Games** and **Media**, switched with **LB** and **RB**, and **Search**, **Settings** and **Power** icons in its top bar. Most of its screens show no button hints. Holding **B** opens its power menu (**Enter Rest Mode**, **Turn Off**, **Restart**, **Quit Universe**), and it's the only look that asks before quitting with a game running.

[Looks](/docs/using/looks/) explains how to switch between them.
