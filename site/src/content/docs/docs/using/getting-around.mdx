Universe Big Screen has four tabs along the top, each with its icon: **Home** (a house), **Library** (a grid), **Media** (photos) and **Settings** (a gear). **LB** and **RB** switch between them. Up from the top of a page reaches the tab bar, with the search glass, the power button and, while a game runs, its badge.

## Buttons and keys

These pages name the buttons by their Xbox letters. The keyboard has a key for each one but **HOME**.

| Pad | Keyboard | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Plays, opens or picks what has the focus. Held on a game, it opens the game menu. |
| **B** | <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Goes back. Held, it opens the power menu. |
| **X** | <kbd>I</kbd> | Opens the focused game's page. |
| **Y** | <kbd>F</kbd> | Adds the focused game to your favourites, or takes it out. |
| **LB** / **RB** | <kbd>Q</kbd> / <kbd>E</kbd> | Previous and next tab. |
| **LT** / **RT** | <kbd>PgUp</kbd> / <kbd>PgDn</kbd> | Previous and next section in Settings and on a game's settings. |
| **Start** | <kbd>F1</kbd> | The menu for what has the focus. On a game, that's the game menu. |
| D-pad, left stick | Arrow keys | Move. |
| Right stick | <kbd>[</kbd> / <kbd>]</kbd> | Scrolls a screenful at a time. |
| **HOME** | none | Opens the dock over a running game ([Playing](/docs/using/playing/)). |

The hint bar along the bottom lists the buttons the focused item takes, drawn as your pad prints them ([Button glyphs](/docs/controllers/supported/#button-glyphs)). Where a button does something else, it says so.

## Search

Press **A** on the search glass in the tab bar. Search finds games and settings as you type.

<Shot name="search" />

**A** on a cover plays the game, and **Start** opens its menu. **A** on a setting opens its page on that row. On the on-screen keyboard, **X** deletes the last letter and **B** closes search. A physical keyboard types straight in.

## Power

Hold **B**, or press **A** on the power button in the tab bar.

<Shot name="power-menu" />

- **Quit Universe** closes Universe and the running game.
- **Suspend** puts the machine to sleep. A running game keeps running.
- **Reboot** and **Power off** ask first, and close the running game.
- **Stay** closes the menu.

## Menus and questions

In a menu, Right opens a row marked ›, Left or **B** goes back, and **Start** closes the menu. A menu that changes a game's setting can also offer **Y**, for all your games. In a question, **B** answers no.

## Mouse and touch

A click or a tap moves the focus, and a second one acts like **A**. Buttons, tabs and menu rows act on the first. Press and hold works like holding **A**, and dragging and the wheel scroll. A right click on a cover opens the game's menu, and a second one closes it; anywhere else it's **B**.

The Switch 2 and PS5 looks are laid out differently: see [Looks](/docs/using/looks/). There, a right click on a game does what pressing and holding it does.
