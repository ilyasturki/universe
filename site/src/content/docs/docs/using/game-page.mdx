## The buttons

- The play button reads **Play**, **Continue**, **Resume** or **Manage install**, as on the home row.
- The heart adds the game to your favourites or takes it out.
- **Update** shows when the game's store has an update and can install it.
- **···** opens the game menu, with the game's settings, media and achievements.

Above the buttons, one line gives your play time and when you last played. It ends in **Update available** when a newer release is out, or **May be out of date** when Universe can only guess. For a game you update yourself, install the update, then choose ··· › Manage › **Mark as current**.

Under the buttons, the store's description sits on the left and its screenshots on the right. **Right** moves into the screenshots, **Up** and **Down** go through them, and **Left** comes back. **A** shows one full screen.

On Switch 2, **Update** and **Mark as Current** sit in the game's options. On PS5 they sit in its **···** menu, and its **Information** page shows the version.

## The game menu

**Start** on a game opens its menu, wherever the game is: Home, Library, search or its page.

<Shot name="game-menu" />

- **Play**, or on the running game **Resume** and **Quit** with its title, which closes it without asking.
- **Details** and **Add to favourites**.
- **Game settings**, everything the game launches with ([Launch settings](/docs/running/launch-settings/)).
- **Achievements**, when the game's store lists some.
- **Media ›**: **Screenshots**, **Recordings** and **Journal**, once the game has some ([Media](/docs/using/media/)).
- **Manage ›**: **Artwork** ([Artwork](/docs/library/artwork/)), **Sessions and logs** ([Troubleshooting](/docs/running/troubleshooting/)), **Data and saves**, **Mark as current** on a game you update yourself, then **Remove from library…**.

## Achievements

**Achievements** lists what the store reports, the unlocked ones first. **X** asks the store again. GOG games unlock theirs while you play; Steam and Epic Games update after each session.

## Data and saves

Manage › **Data and saves** shows the game's saves and backups, the tools of its Wine prefix, and how much space each part takes. [Saves and cloud saves](/docs/library/saves/) covers the saves, [Disk space](/docs/library/disk-space/) the rest.

## Removing a game

Manage › **Remove from library…** asks first. The game leaves every list, but its files, its hours and its journal stay, and its recordings move to **Archived recordings** in Settings › Storage. Install it again from its store, or run [`universe restore`](/docs/reference/cli/restore/) with its name, and it comes back with its recordings.

Removing doesn't uninstall. To free the space, [uninstall it](/docs/library/disk-space/#uninstall-or-remove) instead: the game then stays in the library, not installed.

The Switch 2 and PS5 looks name these menus differently: see [Looks](/docs/using/looks/).
