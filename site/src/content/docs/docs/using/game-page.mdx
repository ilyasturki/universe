## The buttons

- The play button reads **Play**, **Continue**, **Resume** or **Manage install**, as on the home row.
- The heart adds the game to your favourites or takes it out.
- **Game settings** holds everything the game launches with ([Launch settings](/docs/running/launch-settings/)).
- **Media** shows once you have a screenshot, a recording or a journal entry of the game. With one kind, it opens that page; with more, a menu lists them.
- **Achievements** shows when the game's store lists achievements for it.
- **···** opens the game menu.

**Y** toggles the favourite from anywhere on the page.

## Stats, description and screenshots

The stats are **PLAY TIME**, **LAST PLAYED** and **SESSIONS**, and a game you never played shows none. The description and the screenshots come from the store; **A** on a screenshot shows it full screen. Your own screenshots are under **Media**.

## The game menu

**Start** on any game opens its menu, on Home, in Library, in search or on its page. So do **A** held (except on the game's page), the **···** button, **Start** on the running game's badge in the tab bar and, while no game is running, **HOME**. A game still installing has no menu.

<Shot name="game-menu" />

- **Play**, or **Continue** for a game you've played. On the running game, **Resume** and **Quit** with its title (**Quit Dead Cells**) take their place. That Quit closes the game at once, without asking.
- **Details**, the game's page.
- **Add to favourites** or **Remove from favourites**.
- **Achievements**, when the game lists any.
- **Media ›**: **Screenshots**, **Recordings** and **Journal**, each one only when it holds something ([Media](/docs/using/media/)).
- **Manage ›**: **Game settings**, **Artwork**, **Sessions and logs**, **Data and saves**, then **Remove from library…**.

**Artwork** picks the game's pictures ([Artwork](/docs/library/artwork/)). **Sessions and logs** lists every session with how it ended (Quit, Stopped, Crashed with the exit code, Killed) and opens its log ([Troubleshooting](/docs/running/troubleshooting/)).

## Achievements

**Achievements** lists what the game's store reports: the unlocked ones first, newest on top, then the locked ones from the most common to the rarest, with the hidden ones folded into one last row. **X** asks the store again.

GOG games unlock theirs while you play, through Comet ([GOG](/docs/library/gog/#cloud-saves-and-achievements)). Steam and Epic achievements are read from the store after each session.

## Data and saves

Manage › **Data and saves** shows what the game keeps on disk, in four cards:

- **Saves**: where the saves are, **Back Up Now**, **Restore the Latest Backup**, **Export the Backups** (one zip in your home folder), and, for a GOG or Epic game, **Cloud Saves**. When the cloud and this machine disagree, it offers **Keep the Saves on This Device** or **Keep the Cloud's Saves**.
- **Backups**: one row per backup, and **A** restores it.
- **Wine Prefix**, for a Windows game: **Move into Universe's Prefixes**, **Wine Configuration**, **Winetricks**, **Run a Program in the Prefix…**, **Stop the Prefix's Programs** and **Reset the Prefix…**, which backs up the saves first.
- **Storage**: the size of the **Install folder**, **Universe's files**, the **Recordings** and the **Logs**.

[Saves and cloud saves](/docs/library/saves/) covers backups and cloud saves, [Disk space](/docs/library/disk-space/) the rest.

## Removing a game

Manage › **Remove from library…** asks first. The game leaves every list, and its install folder, its hours and its journal stay on disk. Its recordings move to **Archived recordings**, in Settings › Storage. Install the game again from its store and it comes back.

Removing doesn't uninstall. To uninstall, press **A** (**Options**) on the game's row in Settings › **Downloads** and pick **Uninstall…** ([Downloads](/docs/settings/downloads/)); a runner's page in Settings › Emulators & Proton offers the same. The game stays in the library with its hours, as not installed.

## In the other looks

In the Switch 2 look, **Start** on a game, or a long press on its tile, opens **Software Options**: **Start** or **Continue** (**Resume** and **Close Software** while it plays), **Software Information**, **Add to Favourites**, **Achievements**, **Game Settings**, **Recordings**, **Journal**, **Play Log**, **Data Management**, **Artwork** and **Remove from Library…**.

The PS5 look shows the game's hero under the Games row, and further down its **Game Hub**, with **Continue where you left off**, **Trophies**, **Captures**, **Journal**, **About** and **Manage**. **Start** opens its **…** menu, with **Information**, **Trophies**, **Game Settings**, **Media Gallery**, **Journal**, **Play Log**, **Saved Data and Storage**, **Artwork**, **Favourite** and **Remove from Library…**. Achievements are trophies there, graded from Common to Ultra rare.
