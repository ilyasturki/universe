Universe Desktop is the app for the mouse and the keyboard. It shares Big Screen's library and settings, and either app sees a game the other started. [Install](/docs/getting-started/install/) says which packages bring it.

<Shot name="desktop-library" />

## The window

The sidebar lists **All Games**, **Favourites**, **Media** and **Store**, then **Sources** and **Platforms**. Under **Sources**, **Added by hand** holds the games you added or imported, such as from Lutris. The library has a search field (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd>) and a sort menu, whose **Show Hidden Games** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd>) lets you unhide a game.

A click on a card opens the game's page, and a right click opens its menu.

## A game's page

The main button reads **Play** or **Continue**, and **Quit** while the game runs. Beside it are the favourite button, **Game Settings** and **More**, which holds **Artwork**, **Hide**, **Update**, **Uninstall…** and **Remove From Library**. Below come the game's achievements, screenshots, recordings, journal and sessions. **Saves and Storage** opens a page with its saves and backups, its cloud saves, its Wine prefix and the space each of its folders takes. In **Storage**, picking a game opens that page too.

Under the title, **Update available** shows when a newer version is out, and **May be out of date** when Universe can't be sure. Otherwise nothing shows there. **More** has **Update** when the store can update the game. For a game you update yourself, install the update, then choose **More** › **Mark as Current**.

A hide, a favourite or a removal shows a message with **Undo**, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> undoes it too. A removal that fails says why once the message goes, and the item comes back.

## Playing

**Play** starts the game on the window's monitor, in gamescope by default. While it runs, **Now Playing** at the foot of the sidebar has **Show Game**, **Screenshot**, **MangoHud**, **Frame Rate Limit** and **Quit Game**. There's no dock and no Pause on HOME, but your pad's macros work. Closing the window closes the game too, after asking.

## Media and the Store

**Media** shows your screenshots, recordings and journal entries. **Store** groups your store games under **In Progress**, **Updates**, **On This PC**, **Not Installed** and **In the Store**.

## The main menu and Preferences

The main menu (<kbd>F10</kbd>) has **Rescan Library**, **Library Artwork…**, **Open Big Screen**, **System Check**, **Storage**, **Preferences** and **What's New**.

**Preferences** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd>) has the same settings as Big Screen's **Stores**, **Controller**, **Game Defaults**, **Emulators & Proton** and **Features** ([Settings](/docs/settings/)). **Controller** also picks a model with no pad connected, so you can bind its macros without it.

**Add Games** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>) has **First-Run Setup…**.

## GNOME

On GNOME, the Shell's search finds your games, and picking one opens it in Universe Desktop and starts it if it's installed. The Arch, Fedora and Nix packages set this up; the installer can't.

The Universe Shell extension lets Universe record or screenshot one window, show its messages and hide the cursor while you play. [`universe setup`](/docs/reference/cli/setup/) installs it ([After installing](/docs/getting-started/install/#after-installing)), and so does **System Check** › **System Setup** › **Set Up**.
