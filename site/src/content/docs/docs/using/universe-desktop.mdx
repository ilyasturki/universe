Universe Desktop is the GTK app for the mouse and the keyboard. It shares Big Screen's library and `config.toml`, and either app sees a game the other started. It needs GTK 4.22 and libadwaita 1.9; [Install](/docs/getting-started/install/) says which packages bring it.

## The window

The sidebar lists **All Games**, **Favourites**, **Media** and **Store**, then **Sources** and **Platforms**. Below 730 px wide it folds away, and <kbd>F9</kbd> brings it back.

The library has a search field (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd>) and a sort menu (last played, title, **Newest**, **Oldest**, **Most Played**). The same menu's **Show Hidden Games** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd>) shows the hidden games, and only them, so you can unhide one.

A click on a game's card opens its page. An empty library offers **Add Games…** and **Get Started**.

## A game's page

The main button reads **Play** or **Continue**, **Quit** while the game runs, and **Not Installed** when there's nothing to start. Beside it are the favourite button, **Game Settings** and **More**.

Below, the page groups:

- **Your Play**, with **Achievements**, **Screenshots**, **Recordings**, **Journal** and **Sessions and Logs**;
- **Saves and Storage**, with cloud saves, **Back Up**, the backups to restore or export, and the Wine prefix's tools;
- **About**;
- **Gallery**, the store's pictures.

**More** holds **Artwork**, **Open Install Folder**, **Hide** or **Unhide**, **Update**, **Uninstall…**, **Remove From Library** and **Remove With Wine Prefix…**.

A right click, a long press or ⋮ on a card opens the game menu, with **Play**, **Details**, **Game Settings**, the favourite and the actions of **More**, minus what doesn't apply. <kbd>Delete</kbd> removes the game whose page is open.

Hiding, favourites and removals (of a game, a screenshot, a recording or a journal entry) show a message with **Undo**, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> undoes them too. **Uninstall…** and **Remove With Wine Prefix…** ask first instead.

## Playing

**Play** starts the game on the window's monitor, in a gamescope of its own while the **Gamescope** setting is on, as it is by default. There's no poster, so the game's window stays black until the game draws. **Play** on another game asks whether to quit the first, with **Quit and Start**.

While a game runs, **Now Playing** sits at the foot of the sidebar. Its popover has **Show Game**, **Screenshot**, **MangoHud**, **Frame Rate Limit** (**Match the Display**, **No Limit** or a number) and **Quit Game**. MangoHud and the limit change this session alone.

There's no dock, no Pause on HOME and no volume or output control, but the pad's macros work while the game runs. After a crash, a notification points to **Sessions and Logs** on the game's page.

Closing the window while a game runs asks first, since the game closes with the app.

## Media and the Store

**Media** shows your screenshots, recordings and journal entries, with **All**, **Screenshots**, **Recordings** and **Journal** to narrow them, and **Delete** and **Open With…** on each ([Media](/docs/using/media/)).

**Store** groups your store games under **In Progress**, **Updates** (with **Update All**), **On This PC**, **Not Installed** and **In the Store**. One job runs at a time. With no store signed in, it offers **Set Up Stores**.

## The main menu

The main menu (<kbd>F10</kbd>) has **Rescan Library**, **Library Artwork…**, **Open Big Screen**, **System Check**, **Storage**, **Preferences**, **Keyboard Shortcuts**, **What's New** and **About Universe Desktop**. The Store page adds **Find Installed Games**. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Q</kbd> quits.

**Open Big Screen** is greyed out while a game runs or while the Big Screen it opened is still up.

## Preferences

**Preferences** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd>) has **Stores**, **Controller**, **Game Defaults**, **Emulators & Proton** and **Features**, plus **Performance** on a Steam Deck, under a search field. They hold the same settings as Big Screen's sections of those names ([Settings](/docs/settings/)).

- Each setting says where its value comes from: this game, its runner, your global value, Universe's default or a value Universe found.
- **Stores** and **Features** end with **Get more…**, for add-ons others made. Themes are Big Screen's alone.
- **Controller** has **Test the Buttons**, **Set Up the Buttons**, **Nintendo Games** while a pad is connected, **Hold Length**, **Volume Step** and **HOME Opens Universe**. While Big Screen is reading the controllers, the test and the setup wait until it closes.

## First run and What's New

On the first start with an empty library, the [first-run setup](/docs/getting-started/first-run/) opens in a dialog, without the Wi-Fi step. The two apps share it, so it runs once, in whichever you open first. **First-Run Setup…**, in **Add Games** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>), runs it again.

**What's New** opens by itself on the first start after an update; **Show what's new after an update**, in Preferences › Game Defaults, turns that off. **About Universe Desktop** has a troubleshooting section that saves a `universe-desktop-debug.txt` report.

## GNOME search

On GNOME, the Shell's search finds your games by title, developer, publisher or platform, hidden ones aside. Picking one opens its page in Universe Desktop and starts the game if it's installed.

The Arch, Fedora and Nix packages install the search provider. After `install.sh` the Shell's search lists no games, since GNOME doesn't look in `~/.local/share`, where the installer script puts it.

## The GNOME Shell extension

The Universe extension for GNOME Shell (versions 45 to 51) lets Universe list and focus game windows, record a single window or take its screenshot, show the volume level and messages, and hide the cursor while you play. Without it, a window recording falls back to the whole screen.

[`universe setup`](/docs/reference/cli/setup/) installs it and turns it on, and it loads at your next login ([After installing](/docs/getting-started/install/#after-installing)). Universe Desktop has the same step under **System Check** › **System Setup** › **Set Up**, and System Check says when the extension is missing, turned off or not loaded yet.
