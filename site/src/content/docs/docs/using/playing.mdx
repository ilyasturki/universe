**A** on a game plays it, and one game runs at a time. Over a running game, **HOME** on your controller opens the dock and holding **HOME** goes back to Universe. The dock needs Big Screen running full screen, as it starts by default ([Where the dock works](/docs/using/playing/#where-the-dock-works)).

## Launching

The game's poster stays until the game's window is up. Presses do nothing meanwhile except **B**, which drops the poster once the game has started and lets it load behind Universe. **HOME** opens a small dock with **Home** and **Quit** alone.

A game whose runner isn't installed yet, such as a Proton build or an emulator, asks first, with **Install and play** and **Not now**. A failed launch says why, in a message starting "Could not launch".

Playing another game while one runs asks to quit the first, with **Quit and start** and **Keep playing**.

## The dock

<Clip name="home-over-game" />

The game's card at the left reads **PLAYING** or **PAUSED**, and **REC** with the time shows at the top right while the session is recorded. A second **HOME**, or **B**, closes the dock.

The buttons, left to right:

- **Resume** closes the dock.
- **Home** brings Universe up, the game behind it, frozen while Pause on HOME is on.
- **Quit** asks first, with **Quit** and **Keep playing**. Unsaved progress is lost.
- **Game** opens the game's **Details**, **Journal**, **Recordings** or **Sessions** page in Universe.
- **Screenshot** takes a screenshot ([Screenshots](/docs/using/playing/#screenshots)).
- **Controllers** lists your controllers, then **Find controllers** ([Controllers](/docs/using/playing/#controllers)).
- **Achievements**, when the game has some, opens them in the tray under the dock.
- **Performance** holds **MangoHud**, **FPS limit**, **Filter**, **Sharpness** (while Filter is on FSR or NIS) and **Pause on HOME**.
- **Sound** holds **Volume** and **Output**.
- **System**, on a machine Universe can tune such as a Steam Deck, holds **Brightness**, **Refresh rate**, **Power limit**, **GPU clock** and **SteamOS fan curve**.

Left and Right walk the row or change a value, and Up and Down move through an open card. **B** closes the card, then the dock. **X** takes a screenshot from anywhere in the dock. A click on the game behind it, or a right click, counts as **B**.

Down from the row raises a tray of the game's screenshots, this session's first. **A** shows one full screen and **Y** trashes it, after a question. **LB** and **RB** switch the tray to the achievements when the game has some. A GOG game's unlocks also show as a banner at the top right while you play.

## HOME

- Pressed over the game, HOME opens the dock. With Universe on screen, it goes back to the game. With no game running, it opens the focused game's [game menu](/docs/using/game-page/#the-game-menu).
- Held over the game for 600 ms, it goes straight to Universe's Home. **Hold length (ms)**, in Settings › **Controller**'s advanced rows, sets the hold, from 400 to 1000.
- With another app in front, HOME brings Universe to the front. **HOME opens Universe**, in the same advanced rows, turns that off.
- No macro can take HOME, and a keyboard has no key for it, so the dock needs a controller.

Back on Home, the game's last frame shrinks into its tile and the tab bar shows a badge with its title and time. **A** on the tile, the badge or the hero's **Resume** returns to the game.

## Pause on HOME

**Pause on HOME**, on by default, freezes the game while the dock or Universe covers it, and a recording pauses with it. A game still loading never freezes.

Turn it off for a game that must keep running, such as online play. The game then runs on behind the dock and reads every button you press there.

The dock's switch sets the running game's own value. **Y** there gives it to every game without a value of its own. The default for every game is in Settings › [Game defaults](/docs/settings/game-defaults/), and each game's in its **Game settings**.

## Screenshots

Screenshots need the **Screenshots** feature, off until you turn it on in Settings › **Features** ([Screenshots](/docs/add-ons/screenshots/)). Without it, the dock answers **Screenshot failed**. Then take one:

- with **Screenshot** in the dock, or **X** anywhere in it;
- with a pad button bound to the Screenshot macro in Settings › Controller. [Some pads ship with one](/docs/controllers/macros/#shipped-macros);
- with [`universe screenshot`](/docs/reference/cli/screenshot/) in a terminal.

A shutter sounds and the screen flashes white. [Media](/docs/using/media/#screenshots) says where the file lands. Inside Big Screen's own gamescope, the shot holds the game alone, without MangoHud, until you turn off **The game's window alone, not every monitor** in the feature's settings.

## MangoHud, the frame rate limit and scaling

Apart from **Pause on HOME**, the dock's **Performance** card changes the running game alone, and its next launch starts from its settings again. Keep a value in the game's **Game settings**, or for every game in Settings › Game defaults ([Gamescope, HDR and frame rate](/docs/running/gamescope/)).

- **MangoHud** shows or hides the overlay. Nothing draws it if MangoHud isn't installed.
- **FPS limit** reads **Auto** (the refresh rate the game sees; no limit for an emulator), **None**, or a rate from the screen's refresh down to 30. It holds with the overlay hidden.
- **Filter** sets how gamescope scales the game: **Default**, **Linear**, **Nearest**, **FSR**, **NIS** or **Pixel**.
- **Sharpness**, for FSR and NIS, runs from **0 · sharpest** to **20 · softest**, or **Default**.

A pad's **Toggle MangoHud** macro shows or hides MangoHud the same way, for this session alone ([shipped macros](/docs/controllers/macros/#shipped-macros)).

## Volume and output

On **Volume**, Left and Right step the level, 2 % at a time; **Volume step (%)**, in Settings › Controller's advanced rows, changes the step. Right also unmutes, and **A** mutes.

**Output** switches to a sound device once you rest on it for half a second.

A pad's **Volume up**, **Volume down** and **Mute** macros work with the dock closed too, and show the level over the game ([shipped macros](/docs/controllers/macros/#shipped-macros)).

## Controllers

**Controllers** lists each controller Universe reads, with **Bluetooth** or **USB** and its charge when known. **A** on one reconnects it:

- A Bluetooth pad is disconnected and connected again. One that doesn't answer has switched itself off; press its PS or HOME button to wake it.
- A pad on a cable can't be reconnected from software. Unplug it and plug it back in.

A game using [Virtual pads](/docs/add-ons/virtual-pads/) sees its pads unplugged and plugged back in, and each takes back its player.

**Find controllers**, the last row, reopens every pad and retries the devices Universe passed over. Use it when a pad in your hands isn't listed. In this look the two exist only in the dock; [`universe controller reconnect`](/docs/reference/cli/controller/) reconnects pads from a terminal.

## Quitting

The dock's **Quit** asks first. **Quit** with the title, in the game menu, doesn't. Quitting Universe or logging out of the Universe session closes the game too, as do the pad's **Stop the game** macro (a hold, bound to no button at first) and [`universe stop`](/docs/reference/cli/stop/).

Universe comes back first, the tile reading **QUITTING**, and the game is asked to close. One that doesn't is asked again after 3 s and stopped 5 s later. Whether it saves on the way out is up to the game.

After a crash, or when the system kills the game, a message points to its log under **Sessions and logs** on the game's page.

With **Back up after playing** on in Settings › Game defaults, the game's saves are backed up when the session ends.

## Where the dock works

The dock, the screenshot flash and the volume level over the game need Big Screen full screen in its own gamescope, as it starts by default. Elsewhere:

- **Big Screen on the desktop**, in a window (`--windowed` or `--size`) or because gamescope failed to start, has no dock. HOME brings its window up over the game. A game frozen by Pause on HOME stays frozen until you resume it from Universe, even if you switch to it from the desktop.
- **Under [Steam's Game Mode](/docs/getting-started/steam-deck/#steams-game-mode)**, HOME over a game stays Steam's. Steam owns MangoHud, the frame rate limit, the volume, screenshots and power, and the pad's macros for those do nothing.
- **Games started from Universe Desktop or `universe play`** get no dock. The pad's macros still work.
- **An add-on theme** draws no dock. Holding HOME brings the launcher back.

## In the other looks

The PS5 look opens its **Control Center** over the game: cards for the game, its **Game Hub**, its trophies and your captures, over a bar with **Home**, the game (**Resume**, **Game Hub**, **Pause on HOME**, **Close Game**), **Screenshot**, **Controllers**, **Trophies**, **Performance**, **Sound**, **System** and **Power**. □ takes a screenshot from anywhere in it. While the game loads, only **Home** and **Power** show.

The Switch 2 look has no overlay. **HOME** over a game flips to its HOME menu and back. On the running game's tile, **A** resumes, **Start** opens its options and **X** closes it, after a question. It has no MangoHud, frame rate or volume controls of its own, though the settings and the pad's macros still apply. Its **Controllers** page, on the HOME menu, lists each pad with **Reconnect**, then **Find Controllers**.
