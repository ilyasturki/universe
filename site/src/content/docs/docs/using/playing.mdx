**A** on a game plays it, and one game runs at a time. Over a running game, **HOME** on your controller opens the dock, and holding **HOME** goes back to Universe.

## Launching

The game's poster stays up until its window shows. **B** lets the game load behind Universe instead. A game whose runner isn't installed yet offers **Install and play**, and a launch that fails says why. Playing a second game asks to quit the first.

## The dock

<Clip name="home-over-game" />

The buttons, left to right:

- **Resume** closes the dock.
- **Home** brings Universe up, the game behind it.
- **Quit** closes the game after asking. Unsaved progress is lost.
- **Game**, drawn with the game's art, opens the game's details, journal, recordings or sessions.
- **Screenshot** takes a [screenshot](/docs/using/playing/#screenshots).
- **Controllers** lists your [controllers](/docs/using/playing/#controllers).
- **Achievements**, when the game has some.
- **Performance** holds [MangoHud, the frame rate limit and scaling](/docs/using/playing/#mangohud-the-frame-rate-limit-and-scaling), and **Pause on HOME**.
- **Sound** holds **Volume**, where **A** mutes, and **Output**, the sound device.
- **System**, on a machine Universe can tune such as a Steam Deck, holds the [Performance](/docs/settings/performance/) rows for this game.

Left and Right walk the row, and Up and Down move through an open card. **B** closes the card, then the dock. Down from the row shows the game's screenshots, this session's first.

## HOME

- Over the game, HOME opens the dock. With Universe on screen, it goes back to the game.
- Held over the game, it goes straight to Universe's Home, where **A** on the game's tile returns to it.
- With no game running, it opens the focused game's [game menu](/docs/using/game-page/#the-game-menu).
- With another app in front, it brings Universe to the front.

A keyboard has no HOME key, so the dock needs a controller.

## Pause on HOME

**Pause on HOME**, on by default, freezes the game while the dock or Universe covers it. Turn it off for a game that must keep running, such as online play: the game then reads every button you press in the dock.

The dock's switch sets it for the running game, and **Y** there for every game without its own. [Game defaults](/docs/settings/game-defaults/) holds the default.

## Screenshots

Turn on the **Screenshots** feature first, in Settings › Features ([Screenshots](/docs/add-ons/screenshots/)). Then press **Screenshot** or **X** in the dock, or a pad button with the Screenshot macro ([some pads have one](/docs/controllers/macros/#shipped-macros)).

## MangoHud, the frame rate limit and scaling

Apart from **Pause on HOME**, the **Performance** card changes the running game until it ends. To keep a value, set it in the game's **Game settings** ([Gamescope, HDR and frame rate](/docs/running/gamescope/)).

- **MangoHud** shows or hides the overlay.
- **FPS limit** caps the frame rate, and **None** lifts the cap.
- **Filter** sets how the picture is scaled to your screen, and **Sharpness** how sharp FSR and NIS make it.

## Controllers

**Controllers** lists each controller with its charge. **A** on one reconnects it, as the focused row says: Universe drops the pad and connects it again. A Bluetooth pad that doesn't come back has switched itself off: press its PS or HOME button. Unplug a pad on a cable and plug it back in. **Find controllers** looks again for a pad that isn't listed.

**Controller settings** brings Universe up on Settings › [Controller](/docs/settings/controller/), the game behind it, to test the pad or set up its buttons.

## Quitting

Save in the game before you quit: whether it saves on the way out is up to the game. If it crashes, a message points to its log under **Sessions and logs** on its page.

## Where the dock works

The dock needs gamescope. It works over a game started from Big Screen full screen in its own gamescope, as it starts by default. Without gamescope, or in a window, HOME brings Universe's window up instead, and a game frozen by Pause on HOME stays frozen until you resume it from Universe. An add-on theme gets no dock. Under [Steam's Game Mode](/docs/getting-started/steam-deck/#steams-game-mode), HOME is Steam's.

It also works over a game started from [Universe Desktop](/docs/using/universe-desktop/#playing) in gamescope. There it has no Pause on HOME, and **Home** brings Universe Desktop's window up.

The PS5 look has its own Control Center over a game, and the Switch 2 look none: see [Looks](/docs/using/looks/).
