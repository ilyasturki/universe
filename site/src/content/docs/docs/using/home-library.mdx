Home is a row of the games you're playing, under the focused game's art. Library holds every game you have.

<Shot name="look-universe" />

## The home row

The row holds the 12 games you played or added last, newest first, and the running game always comes first. A store install in progress leads the row with its percentage. Games an import or a scan brought in join the row once you play them. While fewer than five games qualify, the row shows your library's newest releases instead.

A ♥ marks a favourite, and the running game's cover reads **PLAYING** or **PAUSED**. After the last game, the **Library** tile opens the Library tab.

## The hero

Up from the row reaches the hero, the focused game's logo over three buttons:

- The play button reads **Play**, **Continue** once you have played the game, **Resume** while it runs, and **Manage install** while it installs.
- **Game page** opens [the game's page](/docs/using/game-page/).
- **···** opens the [game menu](/docs/using/game-page/#the-game-menu).

## The Library tab

<Shot name="library" />

Library shows every game but the hidden ones: the games you played or added, latest first, then the others from A to Z. The focused game's name shows in the middle of the tab bar. This look has no sort or filter; [Universe Desktop](/docs/using/universe-desktop/) and the other looks do.

The last cell, **Add a game**, adds a game from a file, a store or Lutris ([Add a game by hand](/docs/library/add-a-game/)). With an empty library, Home offers it too.

## Favourites

**Y** on a game, the heart on its page and the game menu's **Add to favourites** all add it to your favourites or take it out.

## Hidden games

To hide a game from every list and from search, turn on **Hidden** in the **Desktop and library** card of its **Game settings**. Big Screen can't show it again. Universe Desktop can: turn on **Show Hidden Games** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd>), then pick **Unhide** in the game's menu. From a terminal, run `universe set <name> hidden=false`.

The Switch 2 and PS5 looks are laid out differently: see [Looks](/docs/using/looks/).
