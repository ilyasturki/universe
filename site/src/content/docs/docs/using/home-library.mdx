Home is a row of the games you're playing, under the focused game's art. Library holds every game you have.

<Shot name="look-universe" />

## The home row

The row holds the games you have played and the ones you added, by installing them from a store or with **Add a game**. Games a scan or an import brought in (Lutris, emulator folders, store games already on disk) join it once you play them.

- The newest come first, by the later of the last time you played and the time you added the game. Ties read A to Z.
- The running game is always first.
- The row holds 12 games at most. Library has the rest.
- A store install in progress leads the row, its cover marked **INSTALLING** with the percentage.
- While fewer than five games qualify, the row shows the 12 newest games of your library by release year instead.

A favourite's cover carries a ♥. The running game's reads **PLAYING**, **PAUSED** or **QUITTING**. A game with no art shows its title on a dark card ([Artwork](/docs/library/artwork/)).

After the last game, a **Library** tile opens the Library tab. With an empty library, the tile reads **Add a game** and the hero offers **Add a game** and **Set up**, which runs the [first-run setup](/docs/getting-started/first-run/) again.

## The hero

Up from the row reaches the hero, the focused game's logo over three buttons:

- The play button reads **Play**, **Continue** once you have played the game, **Resume** while it runs, and **Manage install** while it installs, which opens Settings › Downloads.
- **Game page** opens [the game's page](/docs/using/game-page/).
- **···** opens the [game menu](/docs/using/game-page/#the-game-menu).

Down or **B** returns to the row.

## The Library tab

<Shot name="library" />

Library shows every game but the hidden ones, in the home row's order, then the games you never played or added, A to Z. The keys work as on the home row.

This look has no sort, no filter and no favourites view. The Switch 2 and PS5 looks have them, and so does [Universe Desktop](/docs/using/universe-desktop/).

The last cell, **Add a game**, opens the Add a game page.

## Add a game

The page has three kinds of rows:

- **Pick a game file…** takes a program or a ROM, and Universe proposes the runner and the title from the file ([Add a game by hand](/docs/library/add-a-game/)).
- One row per store. **A** on a store you're signed in to opens its games in Settings › Downloads; on one you aren't, it opens the store's page to sign in.
- **Import from Lutris** brings in Lutris's games with their hours and artwork, and leaves the games already here as they are.

## Favourites

**Y** on a game, the heart on its page and the game menu's **Add to favourites** all add it to your favourites or take it out.

In this look, a favourite only gets its ♥. The Switch 2 look lists favourites under **All Software** › **Groups**, the PS5 look under **Game Library** › **Favourites**, and Universe Desktop in its sidebar.

## Hidden games

A hidden game stays out of every list in every look, and out of search. To hide one, turn on **Hidden** in the **Desktop and library** card of its **Game settings**.

Big Screen never lists a hidden game, so it can't bring one back. [`universe set`](/docs/reference/cli/set/) can:

```sh
universe set <name> hidden=false
```

So can Universe Desktop. Turn on **Show Hidden Games** in the library's sort menu (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd>), then pick **Unhide** in the game's menu.

A game you removed from the library comes back when you install it again from its store ([Removing a game](/docs/using/game-page/#removing-a-game)).

## In the other looks

The Switch 2 look's HOME row holds the 12 games you played or added last, with no stand-in for a small library. After the games, **View More** opens **All Software**, with two tabs, **Software** and **Groups** (**LB** and **RB**). Software sorts **By Recently Played**, **By Title**, **By Play Time** or **By Release**, and has a search of its own. Groups holds **Favourites**, one group per platform and one per tag.

The PS5 look's Games row starts with a **Welcome** hub, then your recent games, then **Store**, **Media Gallery** and **Game Library**. Under the row, the hero shows **Play Game** (**Resume** while it runs, **View Download** while it installs) and **…**. **Game Library** has **Your Collection**, **Favourites** and **Gamelists** tabs, a **Sort and Filter** menu and **Add Game**.
