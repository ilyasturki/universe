Universe Big Screen comes with three looks: **Universe**, the default, then **Switch 2** and **PS5**. They share your library and your settings.

## Universe

<Shot name="look-universe" />

Dark, with the game's art behind everything: tabs along the top, a hint bar along the bottom and the dock over a running game. The rest of these pages describe this look.

## Switch 2

<Shot name="look-switch2" />

A light look after the Switch 2 HOME menu, with a row of games over a bar of round buttons. Settings is **System Settings**, the media lives in **Album** and **News**, and **HOME** over a game flips to the HOME menu instead of opening a dock. **Font file** in its Appearance replaces its BIZ UDPGothic with a .ttf of your own, the Switch's own font for one.

## PS5

<Shot name="look-ps5" />

After the PS5 home screen: two tabs, Games and Media, a row of tiles over the focused game's hero and Game Hub, and the Control Center over a running game. Most screens carry no button hints, and until Universe has seen a controller the few that do show a DualSense's buttons. **Font file** replaces its Source Sans 3.

## Switching looks

Settings › **Appearance** › **Theme** lists the three looks, then the themes you installed. The switch is live, with no restart. In the Switch 2 and PS5 looks, Appearance shows each look as a row to pick.

<Shot name="settings-appearance" />

Universe remembers the look for the next start, in `~/.local/state/universe/ui-memory.json`. `universe-ui --theme switch2` (or `ps5`, or `universe`) uses one for a single run.

[Appearance](/docs/settings/appearance/) also holds **Sound folder**, **Font file** (Switch 2 and PS5), **Get more…** and **Startup animation**.

## The startup animation

A full-screen start opens on the Universe mark for about two seconds, with a chime unless the sound is muted. Any button skips it.

It doesn't play with a game running, under [Steam's Game Mode](/docs/getting-started/steam-deck/#steams-game-mode), or when Universe starts again after a crash. Settings › Appearance › **Startup animation** turns it off in every look. `universe-ui --no-boot` skips it for one run and `--boot` forces it, but the switch in Appearance wins.

## What's new

The first start after an update opens **What's new**, with the changes of each release since the one you last ran. Settings › [About](/docs/settings/about/) › **What's new** opens it whenever you like, and **Show what's new after an update** there stops it opening by itself ([What's new](/docs/getting-started/update-uninstall/#whats-new)).

## Themes others made

A theme is a look someone else made, installed from **Get more…** in Appearance or with [`universe extension install`](/docs/reference/cli/extension/). It has no dock over a running game; holding **HOME** brings Universe back instead. If a theme leaves you no way out, hold **HOME** on the pad for 5 s to get the Universe look back. [Themes](/docs/add-ons/themes/) covers installing, updating and making one.
