Universe Big Screen comes with three looks: **Universe**, the default, then **Switch 2** and **PS5**. They share your library and your settings, and differ in where things are.

## Universe

<Shot name="look-universe" />

Dark, with the game's art behind everything: tabs along the top, a hint bar along the bottom and the dock over a running game. The rest of these pages describe this look.

## Switch 2

<Shot name="look-switch2" />

A light look after the Switch 2 HOME menu: a row of games over a bar of round buttons.

- **All Software** lists every game, with sorting, search and **Groups** for your favourites.
- Settings is **System Settings**. Downloads is **Updates** there, Network is **Internet** and Storage is **Data Management**. Stores install from **Install** on the HOME bar.
- Screenshots and recordings are in **Album**, the journal in **News**.
- **Start** on a game opens **Software Options**, with its settings, data, artwork and journal.
- There's no dock. **HOME** over a game flips to the HOME menu and back, and on the running game's tile **X** closes it.
- **Controllers**, on the HOME bar, reconnects and pairs your pads.

## PS5

<Shot name="look-ps5" />

After the PS5 home screen: two tabs, **Games** and **Media**, and a row of tiles over the focused game's hero and **Game Hub**.

- **Search**, **Settings** and **Power** are icons in the top bar. Downloads is **Updates** in Settings, and Bluetooth is **Accessories**.
- **Game Library**, at the end of the row, sorts and filters your games. The **Store** tile installs them.
- **HOME** over a game opens the **Control Center**, with the game, screenshots, controllers, performance, sound and power.
- Achievements are trophies, and most screens show no button hints.
- It asks before quitting Universe with a game running.

## Switching looks

Settings › **Appearance** › **Theme** lists the three looks, then the themes you installed. The switch is live, with no restart. In the Switch 2 and PS5 looks, Appearance shows each look as a row to pick.

<Shot name="settings-appearance" />

## The startup animation

A full-screen start opens on the Universe mark, with a chime. Any button skips it, and Settings › Appearance › **Startup animation** turns it off.

## Themes others made

A theme is a look someone else made, installed from **Get more…** in Appearance. [Themes](/docs/add-ons/themes/) covers them, and how to get back to the Universe look when one leaves you stuck.
