Universe Big Screen comes with three looks: **Universe**, the default, then **Switch 2** and **PS5**. They share your library and your settings, and differ in how the screens are laid out and how you move through them.

## Universe

<Shot name="look-universe" />

The look describes itself as "Dark, cinematic: the game's art behind everything." Tabs run along the top (Home, Library, Media, Settings), a hint bar along the bottom names every button you can press, and the dock opens over a running game. It's set in Archivo. The rest of these pages describe this look.

## Switch 2

<Shot name="look-switch2" />

Its description is "The Switch 2 HOME menu." It's a light look, with a row of games on HOME, a bar of round buttons under it and pages that stack on top. Settings is **System Settings**, the media lives in **Album** and **News**, and the hints sit at the right. **HOME** over a game flips to the HOME menu instead of opening a dock. It's set in BIZ UDPGothic, and **Font file** in its Appearance takes a .ttf of your own, the Switch's own font for one.

## PS5

<Shot name="look-ps5" />

In its own words, "The PS5 home screen: the game's world behind a row of tiles." It has two tabs, Games and Media, a row of tiles with the focused game's hero under it and its Game Hub further down, and the Control Center over a running game. Most screens carry no button hints, and until Universe has seen a controller the few that do show a DualSense's buttons. It's set in Source Sans 3, which **Font file** replaces.

## Switching looks

Settings › **Appearance** › **Theme** lists the three built-in looks, then the themes you installed, tagged **Add-on**; one that can't run here reads **Can't be used**. The switch is live, with no restart, and the new look opens on its own Appearance page. In the Switch 2 and PS5 looks, Appearance shows the looks as rows to pick, each with its colours or a screenshot.

<Shot name="settings-appearance" />

Universe remembers the look for the next start, in `~/.local/state/universe/ui-memory.json`. `universe-ui --theme switch2` (or `ps5`, or `universe`) uses one for a single run.

Appearance also holds:

- **Sound folder**, a folder of WAVs that replace the look's own sounds of the same name (`tick.wav`, `enter.wav`, `launch.wav` and the rest);
- **Font file**, in the Switch 2 and PS5 looks;
- **Get more…**, the themes others made;
- **Startup animation**;
- the trademark note.

## The startup animation

A full-screen start opens on the Universe mark, for about two seconds. Thin lines stream toward you, faster and faster, and stop; the mark lands and sends out a ring, then a circle opens from the middle onto the look's home. A quiet chime plays with it unless the sound is muted. Any button skips it.

It plays only when Big Screen starts full screen with no game running. It doesn't play under [Steam's Game Mode](/docs/getting-started/steam-deck/#steams-game-mode), or when Universe starts again after a crash. Settings › Appearance › **Startup animation** turns it off in every look. `universe-ui --no-boot` skips it for one run and `--boot` forces it, but the switch in Appearance wins.

## What's new

After an update, the first start opens **What's new**, with what changed in each release since the version you last ran, newest first. It never opens on the first run. **Show what's new after an update**, in Settings › About and in Game defaults › **Updates**, turns that off. Settings › About › **What's new** opens it whenever you like, and so does **A** on **Version**. **B** closes it.

## Themes others made

A theme is a look someone else made, installed from **Get more…** in Appearance or with [`universe extension install`](/docs/reference/cli/extension/) and its id. It joins the Theme list tagged **Add-on**. An installed theme draws in Universe's main window alone, so it has no dock over a running game (holding **HOME** brings Universe back instead), a plain volume level and a black background. One that fails to load puts the Universe look back and says so, and if one loads but leaves you no way out, holding **HOME** on the pad for 5 s does the same. [Themes](/docs/add-ons/themes/) covers installing, updating and making one.
