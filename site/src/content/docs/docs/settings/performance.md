Settings › **Performance** sets the machine's own controls, such as the brightness and the power limit. It shows only when the machine has a control Universe can set, and never under [Steam's Game Mode](/docs/getting-started/steam-deck/#steams-game-mode). Each row shows only where its control exists:

- **Brightness**: the screen's backlight, on any machine that has one, such as a laptop.
- **Refresh rate**: a Steam Deck's panel, in the [Universe session](/docs/getting-started/universe-session/). A lower rate saves battery.
- **Power limit**: the most the chip may draw, on AMD graphics.
- **GPU clock**, on AMD graphics: **Auto** lets the driver choose, and a fixed clock trades power for steady frame times.
- **SteamOS fan curve**: on SteamOS. Off leaves the fan to the firmware.

A change applies at once, and stays after a restart. Over a game, the dock's **System** button has the same rows for that game alone.

**X** (**Refresh**) reads the controls again.
