Settings › **Stores** lists the stores Universe installs games from, Epic Games, GOG, itch.io and Steam, and any store add-on you install. GOG is the only one on at first.

<Shot name="settings-stores" />

The stores that are on come first, then the **Off** card, then **Get more…** for [add-ons](/docs/add-ons/installing/). A row reads **On**, **Off**, **Set it up** when a setting it can't guess is still empty, or **Unavailable** when a program it needs is missing.

- **A** opens the store's page.
- **Y** turns the focused store on or off in place.

A store's page has three cards:

- **Settings** starts with **Enabled**. Once the store is on, its settings follow, the advanced ones behind **Y**.
- **Game defaults** sets the store's per-game settings, such as cloud saves, for all its games, until a game sets its own.
- **Sign-in** shows the account under **Signed in**, with **Get a sign-in link** and **Enter the code**, or **Get an API key** and **Enter the key**.

The pages for [GOG](/docs/library/gog/), [Steam](/docs/library/steam/), [Epic Games](/docs/library/epic/) and [itch.io](/docs/library/itch/) go through each store's sign-in and settings.

The Switch 2 and PS5 looks turn a store on or off with **X**: see [Looks](/docs/using/looks/).
