Settings is the last tab. Its sections sit in a sidebar under four groups, and the focused section's cards fill the rest of the screen.

## Moving around

- In the sidebar, Up and Down switch the section, and **A** or Right goes into its cards. **B** comes back out.
- **A** acts on the focused row: it flips a switch, runs an action or opens a choice.
- **X** refreshes a section that reads from the system, such as Downloads or Sound.
- **Y** turns the focused store or feature on or off, and shows the advanced rows of Game defaults.
- **Start** opens **More**, a menu of the same actions.
- **LT** and **RT** step through the sections from anywhere.

A number on a sidebar entry counts what waits there, such as the updates on Downloads.

## The sections

Games:

- [Stores](/docs/settings/stores/): which stores are on, and signing in.
- [Downloads](/docs/settings/downloads/): installs, updates and uninstalls.
- [Controller](/docs/settings/controller/): your controller's buttons and macros.
- [Game defaults](/docs/settings/game-defaults/): what every game starts from.
- [Emulators & Proton](/docs/settings/emulators-proton/): the runners and tools.

Extras:

- [Features](/docs/settings/features/): the optional features, such as video capture.
- [Artwork](/docs/settings/artwork/): every game's art, and what's missing.
- [Appearance](/docs/settings/appearance/): the look and the startup animation.

System:

- [Network](/docs/settings/network/): Wi-Fi.
- [Bluetooth](/docs/settings/bluetooth/): controllers and headphones without a cable.
- [Sound](/docs/settings/sound/): where the sound plays.
- [Performance](/docs/settings/performance/): brightness, power limit and the like.
- [Storage](/docs/settings/storage/): what Universe and each game take on disk.

Universe:

- [System check](/docs/settings/system-check/): what Universe needs, and how to fix what's missing.
- [About](/docs/settings/about/): the version, What's new and the first-run setup.

Network shows only on a machine with Wi-Fi, Bluetooth with a Bluetooth adapter, and Performance with a control Universe can set. Under [Steam's Game Mode](/docs/getting-started/steam-deck/#steams-game-mode), Sound, Network, Bluetooth and Performance hide.

The Switch 2 and PS5 looks name some sections differently: see [Looks](/docs/using/looks/).
