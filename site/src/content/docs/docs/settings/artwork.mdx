Settings › **Artwork** shows your whole library's art in one grid, to spot what's missing and fetch it.

<Shot name="settings-artwork" />

There's one row per game and one column per picture: **Box front**, **Square**, **Banner**, **Background**, **Logo** and **Wallpaper**. An empty cell reads "missing", and a picture you picked carries a **Pick** badge. **A** on a cell opens that game's art on that picture ([Artwork](/docs/library/artwork/)).

**Fetch missing art**, Up from the first row, fills every game's empty pictures and missing description, and keeps your picks. **A** while it runs stops it after the game in hand. It reads **Nothing missing** when every game has all six pictures and a description.

A [SteamGridDB key](/docs/library/artwork/#steamgriddb) adds that site's art to the fetch.

The Switch 2 look has no Artwork section: set a game's art from its **Software Options** ([Looks](/docs/using/looks/)).
