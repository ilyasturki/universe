Settings › **Storage** shows what Universe takes on disk, by folder and by game, and what no game holds any more. The sizes can take a few seconds to come in.

<Shot name="settings-storage" />

- **Folders**: each folder Universe writes to, with its size and its drive's free space. Settings › [Game defaults](/docs/settings/game-defaults/) › **Folders** moves the games, prefixes, save backups and recordings.
- **Games**: every game, biggest first. **A** opens its **Data and saves** page.
- **Leftovers**: prefixes no game uses, the recordings and logs of removed games, and the folders of removed games. **A** asks first, then **Move to Trash** sends one to the trash. Trashing a removed game's folder takes it out of your library for good.

**X** (**Refresh**) measures again. [Disk space](/docs/library/disk-space/) covers freeing room.

The Switch 2 look calls this section **Data Management**: see [Looks](/docs/using/looks/).
