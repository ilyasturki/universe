Settings › **Controller** learns a controller's buttons and binds actions to them, the macros. Macros don't fire while this section has the focus.

<Shot name="settings-controller" />

## The controller's card

The card is titled with the controller's model, such as DualSense or Xbox. Its rows:

- A note when something is off, such as **No controller connected**, or **Macros are running in the game session** while a game's session holds the pads.
- **Controller**, with two pads or more, picks the one the card shows.
- **Test the buttons** (A: **Start**) lights each button you press on the pad's picture. Hold **B** for a second, or press **Start** and **Select** together, to finish.
- **Set up the buttons** (A: **Start**) asks for each button in turn, then the sticks, 8 seconds a step. A model never set up gets this offered once.
- **Nintendo games**: where A sits in Nintendo games on every pad of this model. **As printed** (the default) follows the letters on the pad, and the detail line says where that puts A; **A on the right** and **A at the bottom** override it. It writes `controller.layout.<family>` in config.toml, and As printed removes that key. Only the Emulator controls feature reads it, and that feature is off until you turn it on in [Features](/docs/settings/features/).
- One row per button, the extras first (paddles, Fn and the like). The value names the button's macros, such as "Press · Screenshot / Hold · Mute". **Unbound** means Universe has no code for that button yet. HOME opens the menu on a press and goes home on a hold.
- **Advanced** (A: **Show** or **Hide**) opens the two cards below, closed each time the section opens.

While a game's session holds the pads, Test the buttons and Set up the buttons are gone; the Nintendo games row stays.

## A on a button

**A** (**Configure**) opens the button's menu:

- **Learn the button**, or **Learn the button again** once it has a code: press it on the pad. <kbd>Esc</kbd> stops learning.
- **On press…** and **On hold…** pick the action: **Volume up**, **Volume down**, **Mute**, **Screenshot**, **Toggle MangoHud**, **Stop the game** (on hold only), **Key combo…** or **Command…**.
- **Clear press** and **Clear hold**, when the button has one.

HOME only offers learning: its press and its hold are Universe's own.

## Advanced

- **Timing**:
  - **Hold length (ms)**: how long a press lasts before it counts as a hold and fires a hold macro. 400, 600, 800 or 1000; `controller.hold_ms`, 600 by default.
  - **Volume step (%)**: how far a volume macro moves the volume each press. 1, 2, 5 or 10; `controller.volume_step`, 2 by default.
- **HOME**:
  - **HOME opens Universe**: HOME pressed in another app brings Universe to the front; off, HOME does nothing there. `controller.home_summons`, on by default.

## Keys

**X** (**Refresh**) reloads the section.

## From the command line

In config.toml, `[controller]` holds the three settings above, with the learned codes in `[controller.buttons.<family>]` and `[controller.axes.<family>]`, the macros as `[[controller.macros]]` and the Nintendo games choices in `[controller.layout]`.

- [`universe controller`](/docs/reference/cli/controller/) `ls` lists the pads and their buttons, then the macros.
- `universe controller bind`, `unbind` and `learn` do what the button menu does.
- [`universe config set`](/docs/reference/cli/config/) `controller.hold_ms 800` changes a timing.

Testing and setting up the buttons have no command.

[Button setup](/docs/controllers/button-setup/) covers testing and setting up a pad, [Macros and paddles](/docs/controllers/macros/) each action, and [Emulator controls](/docs/controllers/emulator-controls/) the Nintendo layout. To reconnect a pad, or find one Universe missed, use the dock's **Controllers** button over a game ([Playing](/docs/using/playing/)).

## In the other looks

The Switch 2 and PS5 looks call it **Controllers**. On Switch 2 it opens from System Settings or the HOME bar, with **Pair New Controller** at the top, and a **Reconnect** row for each pad and **Find Controllers** after Nintendo games. On PS5 it is in Settings, with **Pair a Controller** after Nintendo games. Universe Desktop has it in Preferences › **Controller**.
