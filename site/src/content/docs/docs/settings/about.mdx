Settings › **About** gives the version and each release's changes, runs the first-run setup again and opens the Power menu.

<Shot name="settings-about" />

## Universe

The section's one card holds:

- **Version**: the version number, with its git revision in parentheses when the build has one. **A** (**What's new**) opens the What's new page.
- **What's new**: the newest release's version. **A** (**Open**) opens the **What's new** page, every version's changes, newest first.
- **Show what's new after an update**: opens that page once, on the first start after an update. It's `desktop.whats_new` in config.toml, on by default, and the same switch as Settings › [Game defaults](/docs/settings/game-defaults/) › Updates.
- **First-run setup** (A: **Run again**): the setup Universe opens with on an empty library. See [First run](/docs/getting-started/first-run/).
- **Power** (A: **Open**): the **Power** menu, which holding **B** also opens. [Getting around](/docs/using/getting-around/#power) lists what it holds.

A release's main changes come with a screenshot or a short clip under their entry. Universe downloads it from the release on GitHub the first time the page shows it and keeps it in its cache. Offline, the entry shows as text alone.

Every release's notes are also in the [changelog](/docs/changelog/).

## From the command line

`universe --version` prints the version. [`universe config set`](/docs/reference/cli/config/) `desktop.whats_new false` keeps the What's new page from opening after an update.

## In the other looks

The Switch 2 look has System Settings › **About**, with **Universe**, **Look** and **Library** rows and no Power row: its power options are the HOME bar's **Power** icon, or **B** held. The PS5 look's Settings › **About** splits into **System Information** and **Setup and Power**, the latter with **First-run setup** and **Power**. Universe Desktop has **About Universe Desktop** and **What's New** in its main menu, the screenshots and clips in What's New alone, and the setup under Add Games › **First-Run Setup…**.
