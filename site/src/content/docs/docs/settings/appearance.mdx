Settings › **Appearance** picks the look, its sounds and its startup animation.

<Shot name="settings-appearance" />

- **Theme**: **A** opens a menu with a screenshot of each look, **Universe**, **Switch 2** and **PS5**, then the themes you installed. A pick switches at once ([Looks](/docs/using/looks/)).
- **Get more…**: themes others made ([Themes](/docs/add-ons/themes/)).
- **Sound folder**: a folder of WAV files that replace the Universe look's sounds of the same name, such as `tick.wav` or `launch.wav`.
- **Startup animation**: the Universe mark when the launcher starts full screen. On by default.

The Switch 2 and PS5 looks list each look as a row, and add a **Font file** row for a font of your own ([Looks](/docs/using/looks/)).
