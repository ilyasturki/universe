Settings › **Sound** picks the output the games, Universe and the desktop play through. It's hidden under [Steam's Game Mode](/docs/getting-started/steam-deck/#steams-game-mode), where the output is Steam's.

## Output

One row per output PipeWire lists: the route, such as speakers, headphones or HDMI, with its sound card as the value. The one in use is tagged **In use**, and the card reads "No output found" when there is none.

**A** (**Use**) on another output makes it the system's default, as the desktop's sound settings would, and switches the card's profile when that output needs it.

## Keys

**X** (**Refresh**) lists the outputs again, after you plug in headphones or a TV.

## From the command line

[`universe output`](/docs/reference/cli/output/) lists the outputs, and `universe output <id>` plays through one. Under Game Mode it refuses with "The sound output belongs to Steam in Game Mode".

Over a game, the dock's **Sound** button has the volume and the same output choice ([Playing](/docs/using/playing/)).

## In the other looks

The Switch 2 look has System Settings › **Sound**, and the PS5 look Settings › **Sound**, with the outputs in an **Audio Output** part. Each output reads its card, then its route, and **Y** refreshes the list. Universe Desktop has no Sound page.
