Settings › **Sound** picks the output the games, Universe and the desktop play through. It's hidden under [Steam's Game Mode](/docs/getting-started/steam-deck/#steams-game-mode), where the output is Steam's.

There's one row per output, such as speakers, headphones or HDMI, and the one in use is tagged **In use**. **A** (**Use**) on another makes it the system's output. **X** (**Refresh**) lists the outputs again, after you plug in headphones or a TV.

Over a game, the dock's **Sound** button has the volume and the same choice of output.
