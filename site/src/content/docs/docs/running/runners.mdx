A runner is what starts a game: Proton or Wine for a Windows game, the runner called Linux for a native game, an emulator such as Dolphin or PCSX2 for a console game. The [runner list](/docs/reference/runners/) has every one.

<Shot name="settings-emulators-proton" />

## A game's runner

A store game runs on Proton unless the store installed a Linux build, and a game found in an emulator's folder gets that emulator. To change it, pick another **Runner** in the **Launch** card of the game's **Game settings**. That's also how a native game that landed on Proton goes back to Linux.

When a game's runner is missing, **Install and play** installs it first.

## Emulators & Proton

Settings › [Emulators & Proton](/docs/settings/emulators-proton/) lists the runners on this machine, then the ones Universe can download, the ones your distribution provides, and the tools. A tag marks one that needs an install or an update.

A runner's page has:

- **Program**, the program Universe found. On an emulator's page you can set your own path, such as a Flatpak's, which Universe doesn't look for.
- **Gamescope**, whether this runner's games get a gamescope of their own ([Gamescope, HDR and frame rate](/docs/running/gamescope/)).
- **Builds**, what Universe can install for this runner.
- The defaults for this runner's games ([Launch settings](/docs/running/launch-settings/#where-each-one-is-set)), and **Games**, which ends on **Add a game…**.

## Builds

Universe installs Proton builds and emulators itself, and [keeps them up to date](/docs/getting-started/update-uninstall/#what-universe-keeps-current). An emulator on your PATH runs first. **A** on a build's row offers what fits: **Follow the newest …**, **Use …** for one build, **Roll back …** after an update that misbehaves, or **Install another version…**.

gamescope, MangoHud and gpu-screen-recorder come from your distribution. Their **Install from the distribution** installs one where your system allows it.

## Proton builds

**Proton build** names the build a game runs with. Its default, `proton-ge`, takes the newest GE-Proton on the machine, whether Universe, Steam, Lutris or Heroic installed it, and downloads one at the first launch when there's none.

Set the default on Settings › Emulators & Proton › **Proton**, and one game's in the **Proton** card of its Game settings ([Another Proton build](/docs/running/game-fixes/#another-proton-build)).
